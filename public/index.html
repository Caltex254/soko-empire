OUR STORY

Soko Empire is for people who know personal style is not about how much you spend. We source distinctive pre-loved pieces and make them easy to discover, shop and enjoy — wherever you are in Kenya.

THE DETAILS

Delivery & returns

Nairobi CBD pickup is free. Same-day Nairobi delivery is KSh 300, subject to order time and rider availability. Countrywide parcels are sent through available courier or parcel partners, with an estimated fee shown at checkout. Confirm your delivery details before payment. Because thrift pieces are often one-of-a-kind, contact us promptly if an item arrives damaged or materially different from its listing; eligibility and timelines will be confirmed in our final store policy.

Frequently asked questions

Are the clothes new?

Items are pre-loved thrift pieces. Each listing displays its condition grade and available size information.

How do I choose a size?

Check the size shown on the product page and message our team on WhatsApp if you need measurements.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. Countrywide delivery is available through courier and parcel partners, subject to destination coverage.

How do I pay?

Use the M-Pesa Till or Paybill details shown at checkout. Live STK Push will only be available after the shop connects and tests its Daraja account.
